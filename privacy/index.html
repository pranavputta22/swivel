Swivel.

Privacy policy

Swivel collects nothing about you. This page says what the game stores and where.

Effective EFFECTIVE_DATE. This policy covers the Swivel app for iPhone and iPad and the web version of the game.

What we collect

Nothing. Swivel has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you, your device or how you play is sent to us.

Two things can leave your device, and only by your choice: what you share with the Share button, and, if you are signed in to Game Center, your Swivel achievements and scores, which go to Apple. Both are described below.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your progress: the stars for each level, daily results, your album and your endless counts.
  • The levels you draw.
  • Your settings, such as sound and haptics.
  • If the game ever fails, a short note of what failed: the time, the version of the game and the error. It holds nothing about you and stays on your device unless you copy it and send it to us yourself.

This stays on your device. Reset in the game's Settings clears your progress; deleting the app removes everything.

iCloud

If iCloud is on for Swivel, the app also keeps your progress in your own iCloud, so it reaches your other devices. That copy is in your Apple Account. We cannot see it. You can turn this off in the Settings app, under your name, iCloud.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app gives Apple your Swivel achievements and three scores (your stars, your best daily streak and the endless boards you have cleared), so that they appear on your Game Center profile and its leaderboards. Game Center is Apple's service and Apple's privacy policy covers it. We receive none of it. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent.

Sharing

When you share a result or a level, the game hands a line of text and a link to your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The link holds only the date of the board or the layout of the level. It says nothing about you.

Reminders

A daily reminder, if you turn it on, is scheduled by the app on your own phone. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

The web version

The web version stores the same things in your browser. It loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for that file; Google's own privacy policy covers that request. The app does not do this: its typeface is inside the app.

Children

Swivel is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
