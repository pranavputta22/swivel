Help with Swivel

Swivel is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. If something is not working, or you have an idea for it, write to us.

Contact: brightfoldapps@icloud.com

We read every message. Please say which device you have and what you were doing when the problem showed up.

How do I play?

Tap a rocket to launch it. It goes if nothing stands between it and the edge of the board. Every launch turns the rockets beside it a quarter turn clockwise. Clear the board. The question mark on the menu shows every kind of rocket.

I am stuck on a level

Hint shows a move that is safe from where you are. If the board can no longer be cleared, the game says so and offers to rewind to the last spot that can still be won. A clear with a hint earns one star; you can always come back for the rest.

How are stars earned?

One star for clearing a level, two for clearing it without a hint, three for clearing it with no hint and no undo. A level keeps its best result.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. If iCloud is on for Swivel, your progress is also kept in your own iCloud, so it follows you to a new iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I move my progress to another phone, or from the browser into the app?

On the phone that has your progress, open Settings, then Move, then Copy my code. On the other phone, or in the app, open Settings, then Move, then Enter a code, and paste it. The two are joined: each keeps what it has and gains what the other has, so nothing is lost on either side.

How do I send a board to a friend?

After a daily board, Share result sends your stars with a link to that day's board. After a level you drew, Share level sends a link to it. The link opens in the app, or in any browser with nothing to install.

The game said "Something went wrong"

Tap Reload. Your stars are safe; only the level you were on starts again. If it happens more than once, open Settings: a line there says a problem was noted. Tap Copy details and send them to the address below, so it can be fixed.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

In the app, Settings, then Daily reminder. If the switch will not stay on, notifications for Swivel are off in the phone's own Settings; tap the switch again and the game takes you there.

How do I start over?

Settings, then Reset. This clears every star, the daily streak, the album and the endless counts.

Does Swivel need a connection?

No. The whole game is on your device.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
